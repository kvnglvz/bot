Criteria

  • Not convicted by final judgment of an offense punishable by imprisonment for a period exceeding six (6) years, or a violation of the Corporation Code of the Philippines.
  • Registered member of IPC within six (6) years prior to the date of election.
  • Confirmed Corporate Member of IPC-AG Inc., meeting the following requirements:
    • Professes faith in Jesus Christ and identifies with the Christian faith.
    • Affirms the mission, vision, fundamental beliefs, and values of IPC.
    • Actively participates in the worship, fellowship, discipleship, and ministry of IPC and is in good standing, including participation in the Annual General Assembly (AGA), for a minimum period of three (3) years, subject to verification and confirmation by IPC.
    • Has publicly declared faith through water baptism.
    • Has completed Apex 1 and Apex 2 classes, or their duly approved equivalent.
    • Has signed the IPC Membership Covenant Form.
    • Has received confirmation of membership from the Board of Trustees.
  • Majority of the Trustees must be residents of the Philippines.
  • Of legal age, at least twenty-one (21) years old.
  • Currently residing in Baguio City at the time of the election.
  • Physically fit to perform the duties and responsibilities of a BOT member.
